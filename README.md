# Airline Route Profitability Cost Analysis
## Introduction
Airline profitability depends on balancing revenue generation, operational efficiency, and strategic resource allocation. This project analyzes flight-level financial data from a simulated Middle Eastern carrier to identify the primary drivers of profitability across routes, demand segments, and aircraft assignments.

The objective is to understand:
1. What factors contribute to profitability differences across route categories?
2. Whether cost structure, revenue generation, or fleet assignment primarily explains financial performance.
3. Whether hidden route segments exist based on financial and operational characteristics.
   
## About the Dataset
The dataset contains approximately 7,500 flight records representing a full year of operations across 30 routes originating from Dubai (DXB).

Each flight contains operational, revenue, cost, and profitability information, including:
- Route characteristics
- Aircraft assignment
- Passenger demand
- Revenue components
- Operating costs
- Profitability metrics

The dataset includes:
- 30 destinations
- 6 aircraft types
- Short-, medium-, and long-haul routes
- High and medium demand classifications
